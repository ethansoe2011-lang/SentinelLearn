Cybersecurity Core Concepts

Learn about common digital security risks and defensive best practices.

🎣 Phishing & Social Engineering

  • Spear Phishing: Highly targeted attempts addressing specific individuals or organizations.
  • Red Flags: Urgency, unexpected attachments, altered URLs, and requests for sensitive credentials.

🦠 Malware & System Threats

  • Ransomware: Encrypts user files and demands payment for decryption keys.
  • Trojan Horses: Malicious software masquerading as legitimate applications.
Explore Topic Deep Dive →

🌐 Network & Traffic Security

  • Man-in-the-Middle (MitM): Interception of communications over unsecured network channels.
  • Defenses: HTTPS encryption, VPN usage, and strict certificate validation.
