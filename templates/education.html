Cybersecurity Essentials

A quick reference guide for recognizing and neutralizing digital threats. Choose a topic to dive into an interactive learning module.

  • Phishing Elements: Look for extreme urgency, spelling errors, and sender email addresses that don't match the company domain.
  • Deceptive URLs: Always hover over links before clicking. Ensure the spelling of the domain is exact (e.g., google.com vs g00gle.com).

🛡️ How to Prevent Attacks

  • Unexpected Payloads: Treat unsolicited attachments with extreme caution, particularly executable files (.exe) or compressed archives (.zip).
  • Patch Management: Keep your operating system, web browsers, and critical software updated to eliminate known vulnerabilities.
Deep Dive: Interactive Malware Sandbox ➔
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Enable MFA on all accounts. Even if your password is stolen, attackers cannot bypass the secondary token.
  • Zero Trust: Never automatically trust public Wi-Fi. Avoid logging into banking or sensitive portals on unencrypted networks.
